On the shade figure

An earlier draft of this project claimed 99.2% annual shade on the spine. It does not survive a geometric check: a flat 9 m canopy at 5.5 m stops shading a 9 m walkway once the sun is low and to the south, because the shadow slides off the path. Modelled hour by hour across all 4,402 annual daylight hours, the canopy alone gives 60.2%, and the canopy together with its flanking tree avenue gives 69.6%.

The honest number is lower, and it is the better argument — it is what shows the tree avenue is structural to the concept rather than decoration.
