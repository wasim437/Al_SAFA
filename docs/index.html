Al Safa 2 Park — Falaj Al Safa

A 15,000 m² neighbourhood park in Dubai, redesigned around one finding: at summer noon the sun sits almost directly overhead, so shade has to be engineered rather than borrowed. Every figure on this portal is generated from the project's computed outputs — click any tile to see its source.
