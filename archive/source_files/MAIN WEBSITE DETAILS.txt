About the Challenge
This challenge represents a pioneering pilot initiative to harness the power of artificial intelligence across urban planning, architecture, and park design—reimagining the future of parks and public spaces in Dubai. It explores how an integrated ecosystem of AI tools can augment the entire design journey—from site analysis and user insights to concept generation, scenario testing, and solution development—ultimately elevating design quality while accelerating innovation, creativity, and human-centered outcomes.

Objectives & Vision
To unleash creativity through the use of diverse AI tools, while embracing a co-design approach that brings together designers, communities, and stakeholders—enabling the exploration of new possibilities, challenging conventional approaches, and generating innovative, human-centered public spaces.

About the Park and Scope of the Design Challenge

Al Safa Park 2
The Challenge aims to leverage artificial intelligence technologies and tools to reimagine the design of Al Safa 2 Park, developing innovative concepts that are both visionary and implementable. Submissions are expected to include a fully integrated master plan, supported data analyses, as well as visualizations that reflect the user experience. This is complemented by detailed plans and drawings, a human-centered design approach, and practical solutions that enhance sustainability and quality of life.

Expected Outcomes
AI-Integrated Design
Approach

AI used as a supportive partner-enhancing creativity, analysis, and decision making throughout the process. AI tools may be applied to explore design iteration, analyze data, user behavior, and optimize spatial layouts. The focus is not on automation, but how on how AI can strengthen human-centered, sustainable, and context responsive design thinking.

Spatial Intelligence &
Feasibility

A well-structured park layout that organizes paths, activities, and open spaces into a clear and cohesive spatial experience. The proposal should demonstrate strong connectivity, accessibility, and functional zoning, while ensuring the design is practical, buildable, and functional.

Park for All: Human-Centered
Experience

A park design that prioritizes people, comfort, and everyday use, creating inclusive and engaging spaces for all ages and abilities. The proposal should enhance wellbeing, social interaction, and sense of place, reflecting the needs of the community and local identity.

Design Narrative &
Clarity

Environmental Response – optimizing shade, microclimate, and sustainability strategies
User Experience Insights – informing design decisions based on predicted user behavior
Design Optimization – improving efficiency, performance, and functionality
Visualization – producing AI-assisted visuals to communicate design intent

Environmental analysis (shade, heat, solar exposure, wind – at a basic level)
