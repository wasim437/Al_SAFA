 SCHEDULE 1 GENERAL DESCRIPTION OF THE DESIGN CHALLENGE  A. Introduction  Dubai Municipality’s AI Park Design Challenge, is an initiative that explores the potential of artificial intelligence to support, inspire, and enhance the design of public spaces. The Design Challenge is founded on the principle that human experience and community participation should remain at the heart of creating public spaces. While artificial intelligence offers new opportunities for analysis, ideation, and exploration, the role of technology is to augment and empower designers, enabling them to create more inclusive, responsive, and meaningful environments. Participants are invited to demonstrate how artificial intelligence can be employed throughout the design process to better understand people and places, analyze environmental and spatial conditions, generate and test design alternatives, unlock creativity, and support evidence-based decision-making. The design challenge encourages participants to use AI not merely as a visualization tool, but as a catalyst for innovation and creativity, enabling the development of bold ideas, strong concepts, and transformative visions for the future of public spaces. Through this initiative, Dubai Municipality seeks to attract innovative and forward-thinking proposals that: • Place people and communities at the center of design decisions; • Demonstrate meaningful and responsible integration of artificial intelligence; • Promote sustainability, inclusivity, and quality of life; • Celebrate local identity and create a strong sense of place; and • Inspire new approaches to designing the public spaces and cities of the future.  At its core, the challenge is founded on the belief that the best public spaces are shaped by the people who use them. Accordingly, all submissions will be evaluated by an independent jury comprising leading local and international experts from the fields of architecture, urban design, landscape architecture, technology,

The jury will assess submissions against a set of predefined criteria and select a shortlist of outstanding proposals. In a unique celebration of participatory design and community engagement, the shortlisted proposals will then be presented to the public, empowering residents and communities to participate in selecting the winning design. Through this process, the challenge seeks to demonstrate that the future of public space design lies in the intersection of artificial intelligence, human creativity, expert knowledge, and community participation. This challenge aims to use artificial intelligence as a design support and decision-making tool, rather than as an end product or a requirement to incorporate AI technologies within the park itself. Participants are encouraged to leverage AI throughout the design process—including research, site analysis, data interpretation, ideation, scenario testing, visualization, and evaluation—to enhance creativity, inform design decisions, and develop more human-centered, sustainable, and implementable proposals. The focus of the challenge is on how AI can augment the designer's process and contribute to better public space outcomes, not on embedding artificial intelligence features or technologies within the final park design. Level of Submission: Submissions shall include both Concept Design and Preliminary Design proposals, demonstrating the overall vision, spatial organization, key design components, and preliminary development of the proposed park. Detailed design and construction documentation are not required at this stage. The Dubai Municipality AI Design Challenge aims to establish a new model for designing public spaces—one that brings together technology and people to create innovative, inclusive, and future-ready environments, while further reinforcing Dubai's position as a global platform for urban innovation and the design of future cities.  B. About The park  The Design Challenge invites participants to develop a concept and preliminary design proposal for the redevelopment of a 15,000-square-metre neighborhood park in Al Safa 2, Dubai. The project presents an opportunity to reimagine the future of neighborhood parks by creating an innovative, inclusive, and sustainable public space that enhances quality of life, promotes health and well-being, and strengthens community connections. The existing park is planned to be redeveloped into a vibrant, human-centered destination that responds to the evolving needs and aspirations of the community.

 B. Scope of Work  The Design Challenge consists of two design stages: Concept Design and Preliminary Design. Participants are expected to demonstrate how artificial intelligence can be meaningfully integrated throughout both stages to support analysis, ideation, design development, and decision-making while maintaining human creativity and professional judgment. The Design Challenge seeks proposals that demonstrate both a compelling conceptual vision and an appropriate level of preliminary design development. Participants are expected to leverage artificial intelligence as an enabling tool throughout the design process while maintaining human creativity, community participation, and professional judgment at the center of decision-making taking.   Proposals should present innovative, human-centered, and implementable concepts that respond to the needs of future public spaces in Dubai. Participants are also required to consider the project's total implementation budget of AED 35 million and demonstrate the feasibility, scalability, and practicality of their proposals within this financial framework for the redevelopment of the 15,000-square-metre site.
