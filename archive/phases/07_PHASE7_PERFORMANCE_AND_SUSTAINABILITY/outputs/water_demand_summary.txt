PHASE 7.4 - REAL WATER-DEMAND MODEL
=============================================

REAL SOURCED INPUT (Ghaf irrigation, Abu Dhabi field study):
  January: 24.4 L/day/tree | July: 52.8 L/day/tree

DESIGN ASSUMPTIONS (from Phase 5/6 masterplan):
  ~108 native trees across 3,804 sqm of green zones
  2,516 sqm of Paspalum turf lawns

COMPUTED ANNUAL IRRIGATION DEMAND:
  Native trees: ~1,593 m3/year
  Turf lawns:   ~4,109 m3/year
  TOTAL:        ~5,702 m3/year (5,702,000 litres/year)

This gives the design a real, defensible water budget - the kind of feasibility
evidence the competition's evaluation matrix rewards under 'Feasibility &
Implementation' (20%) and 'Sustainability' (20%).
