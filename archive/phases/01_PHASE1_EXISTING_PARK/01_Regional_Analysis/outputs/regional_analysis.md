# Phase 1.01 — Regional Analysis
Al Safa 2 Park | Dubai Municipality AI Park Design Challenge

Scale progression studied: **Dubai → District → Neighborhood → Park**

## 1. Dubai-Wide Context
- The site sits within Dubai's established "First Cluster" of low-rise residential communities west of Sheikh Zayed Road (Al Safa, Al Wasl, Umm Suqeim, Jumeirah), one of the city's oldest planned neighborhood belts.
- Dubai Municipality's own **Neighborhood Parks Manual** (reference document, see `99_SOURCE_FILES`) classifies this typology as a **District/Community/Neighbourhood park**, intended primarily for nearby residents — recreation, nature contact, sport — operating **05:00–23:00, all seasons**.
- Manual benchmark bands (by surrounding population density):
  - High density (>220 person/ha): park area range 3,000–150,000 sqm
  - Medium density (70–220 person/ha): park area range 3,500–300,000 sqm
  - Low density (<70 person/ha): park area range 4,000–500,000 sqm
  - **Al Safa 2 Park = 15,000 sqm**, placing it firmly in the "neighborhood-scale" band regardless of density tier — consistent with low-rise villa/townhouse surroundings typical of Al Safa 2.

## 2. District Level — Al Safa / Umm Suqeim / Al Manara Belt
From the official master-plan location graphic (`Ai Park - Master Plan (4).jpg`), the park sits at the convergence of four named districts:
- **Umm Suqeim First** (west)
- **Al Safa Second** (north-east)
- **Al Manara** (south)
- **Al Qouz Industrial First** (east, across Sheikh Zayed Road)

This is a **low-rise, villa-dominated residential district**, bounded on its east flank by **Sheikh Zayed Road (E11)** — a major arterial that also separates it from the industrial/commercial Al Qouz belt.

## 3. Neighborhood Level — Immediate Surroundings
Direct adjacencies identified from the location map:
- Umm Suqeim Model School for Basic Education (NE, walking distance)
- Dubai Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation Center (N, adjacent)
- Aisha Butti Al Mulla Masjid (SE, adjacent)
- Residential fabric on all sides via local streets (4C, 6C, 6D, 10C, 10D, 31A, 37A, Street 27, Street 35, B29, C14)
- ONPASSIVE Metro Station on the Dubai Metro (Route 2020 / Red Line vicinity) to the east across SZR — a regional connectivity anchor within a ~600–800m radius (walkable but crossing a major highway).

## 4. Park Level
- 15,000 sqm neighborhood park, existing and slated for full redevelopment.
- Existing use pattern (per manual's neighborhood-park archetype): families, sport enthusiasts, picnickers, teenagers, pet owners; peak visitor capacity guideline **150–400 visitors per 10,000 sqm**; typical visit duration **1–3 hours**.
