# Phase 1.08 — Transportation Analysis
Al Safa 2 Park

## Road Hierarchy (from master-plan location image)
| Road | Classification | Relevance to Site |
|---|---|---|
| Sheikh Zayed Road (E11) | Primary arterial / national highway | Forms the park's east-side context edge; major barrier and noise source; not a direct-access road |
| Al Wasl Street | Secondary arterial | Runs along the site's north-west side |
| Al Manara Street | Secondary arterial | Runs along the south side of the district |
| Local streets (4C, 6C, 6D, 10C, 10D, 31A, 37A, Street 27, Street 35, B29-B29, C14) | Local/residential access roads | Provide direct neighborhood access to the park |

## Metro
- **ONPASSIVE Metro Station** located east of the site, across Sheikh Zayed Road.
- Distance appears to be within a nominal ~600–800m straight-line radius based on the map
  graphic scale (approximate — not measured from GIS data), but SZR is a significant
  pedestrian barrier, meaning **walking access from the metro is not currently comfortable
  or direct** without a proper crossing (bridge/underpass) — this is not confirmed to exist
  and should be checked before relying on Metro-based visitor access.

## Bus
- No bus stop locations are visible/labeled in the provided master-plan graphic. Logged as a
  data gap — Dubai RTA bus route data would need to be sourced separately.

## Cycling
- No dedicated cycle infrastructure visible in surrounding streets from the graphic. Brief's
  own park program calls for "bicycle parking and drop-off areas" and "sustainable mobility
  solutions" — implying current external cycling infrastructure is limited/undocumented.

## Walking
- Local street grid is typical low-rise-residential, walkable block pattern; the park is
  embedded within walking distance of surrounding villas directly.
- East-west walkability is constrained by SZR.

## Parking
- No existing dedicated park parking visible/confirmed from available materials. Brief
  requires the design to address "appropriate drop-off, pick-up, and parking arrangements... in
  accordance with local regulations" — meaning current parking provision (if any) is not
  confirmed adequate and should be treated as an open design question, not an existing asset.

## Drop-off
- Not confirmed from available materials; logged as a gap pending DWG or site visit.

## Emergency Access
- Not confirmed from available materials; the DWG (once converted) may show service/emergency
  access routes. Logged as a Phase 1.03 dependency.

## Summary
Confirmed context: strong arterial (SZR) and secondary road (Al Wasl, Al Manara) framing,
metro proximity across a highway barrier, walkable residential local-street access. Genuine
gaps: bus routes, existing parking capacity, drop-off zones, cycling infrastructure, emergency
access — all logged for follow-up rather than assumed.
