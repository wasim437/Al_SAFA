# Phase 1.12 — AI Analysis
Documentation of how AI was used to assist Phase 1 (Existing Site Understanding)

This log exists to satisfy the Brief's explicit requirement (Schedule 1, Section C) that
participants **document** how AI tools were used throughout the design process — starting
from this first phase.

## Tools Used in Phase 1
| Tool | Role |
|---|---|
| Claude (Anthropic) | Orchestration of the analysis workflow; synthesis of written findings from source PDFs/images; SWOT synthesis logic; identification of data gaps vs. available facts |
| Python (pandas, matplotlib, numpy) | Quantitative computation and chart generation |
| pvlib (NREL Solar Position Algorithm) | Exact astronomical solar position (azimuth/elevation) computation for the site's precise lat/lon — used for Climate Analysis (1.05) and Shadow Analysis (1.06) |
| Pillow / numpy (image processing) | Color-threshold pixel analysis of the master-plan graphic to estimate visible green-canopy footprint and site bounding box |

## Areas of Application (mapped to Brief's "Areas of Application" list)
1. **Site data analysis and interpretation** — automated extraction from the master-plan
   image (green-footprint detection) and structured compilation of brief/manual text into
   the Regional, Urban Context, and Existing Park analyses.
2. **Mapping and visualization of spatial information** — sun-path polar diagram, shadow
   direction plan-view diagram, wind rose, SWOT matrix — all generated programmatically
   rather than hand-drawn, ensuring the underlying numbers are traceable and reproducible.
3. **Environmental analysis** — solar radiation, temperature, humidity, wind, and shadow-
   casting were computed using real astronomical formulas (not estimated by eye), giving an
   evidence base for later shade/comfort decisions.

## Role of Human Judgment (this session)
- AI (Claude) was directed by the user's explicit phase structure and file scope — it did not
  invent the 10-phase framework or the analysis categories; those were provided by the human
  lead and simply organized/executed against.
- Every claim in the Phase 1 documents is tagged as either (a) sourced directly from the
  competition brief/manual/image, (b) computed via a verifiable formula/library, or (c) an
  explicitly flagged data gap — this distinction was a deliberate human instruction ("don't
  invent data") that the AI followed throughout.
- No design decisions (zoning, layout, planting choices, etc.) were made in this phase —
  consistent with the human's direction to keep Phase 1 to analysis only.

## Reproducibility
All charts/tables in Phase 1 were generated by scripts in `01_PHASE1_EXISTING_PARK/_scripts/`
and can be re-run to regenerate identical outputs, satisfying the Brief's ask for an
"AI methodology and workflow" that shows genuine, traceable process rather than a black box.
