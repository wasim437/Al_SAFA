# Opportunities & Constraints Summary
(Companion to SWOT — Phase 1.11)

## Constraints (hard facts that limit design freedom)
1. Fixed site area: 15,000 sqm — no expansion possible.
2. Fixed budget ceiling: AED 35,000,000 implementation cost.
3. Sheikh Zayed Road borders the east edge — a permanent noise/physical barrier, not
   resolvable within the site.
4. Submission requires Concept + Preliminary Design only — detailed construction
   documentation is explicitly out of scope at this stage.
5. All winning-entry IP transfers to Dubai Municipality — a legal constraint on downstream
   reuse of the design, not a site constraint, but relevant to how the team should treat
   proprietary methods/tools used.
6. Climate: ~5 months (May-Oct) of extreme heat with near-zero natural shade at solar noon —
   a hard environmental constraint any design must engineer around, not eliminate.

## Opportunities (real, evidence-based, not yet decided how to use)
1. Full redevelopment scope (not retrofit) — every zone can be reprogrammed from scratch.
2. NW prevailing wind pattern — usable for passive ventilation corridors.
3. Winter sun angle (~41° at noon) — usable for solar-responsive seasonal design (e.g.,
   winter sun pockets vs. summer full-shade zones).
4. Adjacent school, mosque, and rehab center — built-in recurring user bases without
   needing to attract them from elsewhere.
5. Metro proximity — a genuine regional catchment opportunity IF pedestrian connectivity
   across SZR is addressed (flagged as currently unresolved, an opportunity, not a fact).
6. High public visibility of the AI Design Challenge — potential to set a citywide precedent,
   which may be a meaningful "success metric" candidate for Phase 3.

This document intentionally stops at listing opportunities/constraints — it does not select
or prioritize a design response. That belongs to Phase 3 (Opportunity & Design Objectives).
