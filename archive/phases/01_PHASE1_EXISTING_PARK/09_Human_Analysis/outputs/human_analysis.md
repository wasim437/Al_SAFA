## Target Parameters (Manual benchmarks for this park type)
| Parameter | Benchmark |
|---|---|
| Duration of visit | 1–3 hours |
| Peak visitor capacity | 150–400 visitors per 10,000 sqm |
| Number of events supported | Small parks (S): 60+/yr, Medium (M): 24+/yr, Large (L): 3+/yr |
| Gross spend per visit | AED 20–80 |
| Leasable commercial area | ~15% of total park area / 2–5 sqm per visitor |

Applying the capacity benchmark to this site's 15,000 sqm: indicative peak capacity range is
approximately **225–600 visitors** (scaling the 150–400/10,000sqm rate to 15,000 sqm) —
this is a benchmark-derived estimate, not a measured figure.

## Office Workers, Students, Visitors
- Not explicitly addressed in the brief; but given adjacency to Umm Suqeim Model School for
  Basic Education (confirmed from master-plan graphic), **students/school-day traffic** is a
  plausible real user group worth carrying into Phase 2/8 even though not explicitly named in
  the brief.
- "Visitors" (non-resident/city-wide) are relevant given this is a flagship AI Design Challenge
  site — likely to draw media/public interest beyond the immediate neighborhood during and
  after the competition period.

## Activity Mapping, Movement, Behavior, Comfort, Needs — Data Status
Genuine behavioral data (movement heatmaps, dwell-time studies, comfort surveys) requires
either (a) an actual site visit/observation study, or (b) sensor/mobile-location data — neither
is available in the provided competition package. This is explicitly one of the areas the brief
asks participants to address using **AI tools** (e.g., simulating user personas, movement
pattern analysis) as part of the "Artificial Intelligence Integration" scope — meaning it is
expected to be *produced* as part of the design process (Phase 4 onward), not sourced as
existing data now.

## What Belongs Here vs. Later
This document captures the **known, evidence-based facts** about who uses/will use the park.
Persona development, journey mapping, and behavior simulation (explicitly AI-assisted per the
brief) are correctly deferred to **Phase 8 (User Experience & Activation)** and **Phase 4.5
(AI-Assisted Exploration)** — flagged here so nothing is duplicated or invented prematurely.
